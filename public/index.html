Platform Capabilities & Rules

Engineered for fair, rigorous evaluations with role-based segregation and anti-cheat measures.

Strict 2-Attempt Engine

Every candidate is restricted to a hard limit of two attempts per topic. After the second submission, the quiz is permanently locked with full historical score tracking.

Distraction-Free Quiz Room

Clean keyboard-friendly interface (keys 1-4, A-D), live countdown timer with auto-submission, question palette, and submit confirmation modal.

Comprehensive Answer Review
